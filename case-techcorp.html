Overview

A B2B SaaS Company Invisible in Search

TechCorp Inc. builds project management software for enterprise teams. Despite an excellent product and a growing sales team, their website was generating fewer than 8,200 organic visits per month — and almost none of those visitors were converting.

Their domain authority sat at 18. Competitors averaged DA 45+. They had 340+ crawl errors, no content strategy, and were spending $80K per month on paid ads to compensate for their organic deficit.

They came to us with a clear brief: make SEO our most scalable and cost-effective growth channel within 6 months.

The Challenge

Three Problems Compounding Each Other

Our initial audit identified three distinct problem layers, each making the others worse:

1

Technical Debt

340+ crawl errors, 2,800 duplicate content instances, canonical tag misconfigurations, and a 6.8-second average page load time were preventing Google from properly indexing the site.

2

No Content Foundation

The blog had 12 posts — none optimised for search intent, none internally linked, and none targeting the keywords that enterprise buyers were actually using in their search queries.

3

Zero Authority

DA 18 with only 48 referring domains — most from low-quality directories. Google had no reason to trust TechCorp's content over established competitors with hundreds of quality backlinks.

Our Strategy

A Phased Approach to Compounding Growth

We designed a 3-phase programme where each phase built on the last — so results would compound, not plateau.

1

Phase 1 — Technical Foundation (Months 1–2)

Fixed all crawl errors, consolidated duplicate content, implemented canonical tags, rebuilt site architecture, and achieved sub-2s load times on all key pages. Prepared the ground for content and links to have maximum impact.

2

Phase 2 — Content Clusters (Months 2–5)

Built 8 topical clusters around buyer-intent keywords. Produced 24 long-form pillar pages and 96 supporting articles. Every piece was mapped to a specific funnel stage and buyer persona.

3

Phase 3 — Authority Link Acquisition (Ongoing)

Secured 140+ editorial backlinks from SaaS review platforms, industry publications, and partner sites. DA grew from 18 to 46 over the campaign. Links were built to pillar pages, boosting entire cluster rankings.
